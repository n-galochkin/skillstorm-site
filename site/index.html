Questions

When does it come out?

There is no date yet. Next come a Steam page and a playable demo; follow the devlog to catch both.

Which platforms?

PC through Steam, and Android. One game, one progression, controls made for each.

How much will it cost?

On PC, early access at a modest price. On Android, free to play — with no pay-to-win.

Is it PvP?

No, and we say so plainly: your rivals are bots that play like people, with names, builds and gear of their own. That means no queues and no cheaters — a match starts the moment you want one.

What does “auto-battle” mean here?

Archero-style: your character attacks on its own; you control movement and choose skills on level-up. It is not a unit-placing autobattler.

Can I play with friends?

Each player builds a kingdom of their own, and friends are allies. Shared runs are planned.

Skillstorm

Auto-battle royale roguelite

Land with nothing.
Leave the last one standing.

You steer — your weapon fires on its own. Ten to fifteen rivals, a storm closing in, six-minute matches, and the island goes to whoever is left.

FIGHT

How it plays

Move to live.
Stand still to strike.

You only steer. Stop and your weapon fires by itself; move and it holds. Every fight is the same choice: finish the kill, or get out of the arrows.

BUILDS

Builds

Two skills fuse into a title

Every level-up offers three skills. The right pair fuses into a combo title, and titles fuse again into deeper ones: 26 skills, 35 titles.

STORM

The zone

The storm picks the battlefield

Fog and lightning eat the island’s edge and the circle keeps closing. Roaming bosses and mob packs guard the best loot, so the middle of a match never goes quiet.

FORGE

Between runs

From a linen shirt to king of the archipelago

Every island you take pays out: resources, gear, rare finds. Forge your weapon, complete your armour, sail for meaner islands. The kingdom you build is yours alone.
